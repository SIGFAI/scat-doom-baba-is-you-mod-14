# Credits - SuperIntelligent Cat (Doom / GZDoom mod)

Code, rule system, HUD and sprite renders: made for this mod. No assets from commercial games.

## 3D models (rendered into Doom sprites)
- **Kenney Cube Pets** (animal-cat, animal-pig, animal-cow, animal-chick and their animations, colormap texture): CC0 1.0, by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/cube-pets). Rendered in Blender into 8-angle sprites; the cat recoloured ginger with green eyes and given modelled glasses and a graduation cap; an egg modelled for the chickens.

## Fonts
- **Kenney Fonts** ("Kenney Pixel Square", used for the rule word blocks and the HUD font): CC0 1.0, by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/kenney-fonts).

## Effects
- **Kenney Particle Pack** (smoke, circle/light, star and spark particles, pixelated into the poof, XP orb and sparkle sprites): CC0 1.0, by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack).
- Block chips: cut from the Minetest textures below.

## Textures
- **Minetest Game textures** (the 16x16 pixel blocks in `mod/mctex/<block>.png`): CC BY-SA 3.0, Copyright (C) 2010-2023 the Minetest Game texture authors: celeron55, Perttu Ahola <celeron55@gmail.com>, Cisoun, G4JC, VanessaE, RealBadAngel, Calinou, MirceaKitsune, Jordach, PilzAdam, jojoa1997, InfinityProject, Splizard, Zeg9, paramat, BlockMen, sofar, Neuromancer, Gambit, asl97, KevDoy, Mito551, GreenXenith, kaeza, kilbith, tobyplowy, CloudyProton, TumeniNodes, Mossmanikin, random-geek, Extex101, An0n3m0us, Lopano (license text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Source: https://github.com/minetest/minetest_game. Pixel blocks scaled, some tinted or composited (for example ore overlays on stone, netherrack/nether brick/terracotta/deepslate/lapis/quartz-style recolours, liquid animation frames). Because of the ShareAlike licence, the modified block images are also released under CC BY-SA 3.0.
- **Kenney Voxel Pack** (`clouds.png`, used to build the blocky sky `mod/mctex/sky.png`): CC0 1.0, by Kenney (https://kenney.nl). Pixelated and nearest-scaled onto a stepped blue gradient.
- **Freedoom** (the pixelated originals `mod/mctex/px_*.png`: switches, exit signs, computer screens, key doors, teleporter pads, paintings and see-through grates/bars): BSD-style licence, (c) the Freedoom contributors (https://freedoom.github.io). These images are derived from Freedoom's own textures in freedoom2.wad, reduced to 1/4 resolution with nearest-neighbour sampling.
